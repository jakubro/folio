# North

- **Status:** active
- **Owner:** Tove Brann
- **Calibrated:** 2026-04-12

The original station, on the summit cairn. Its rain gauge sits in the lee of the hut, so it reads a little high in a westerly.

## Instruments

| Instrument | Height | Interval |
|---|---|---|
| Anemometer | 10 m | 10 min |
| Rain gauge | 1 m | 10 min |
| Thermometer | 2 m | 1 min |

## Access

The track is passable on foot all year; bring the hut key from the valley office.
